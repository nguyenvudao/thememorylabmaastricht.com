How to use your PHO-CAM

  • SNAP — or press Space — takes a photo. ⏱ 3s gives you time to pose.
  • 🎞 film recolours your next shots: PHO (warm), 1975 (faded), B&W, VIVID.
  • 💬 caption idea writes a pho pun on the newest print.
  • Drag a print into a frame, or simply tap the frame.
  • Write on the caption. Flip ↻ for a note on the back.
  • Double-click a print to zoom in and save it on its own.
  • Click a frame's border to re-crochet it in a new colour.
  • + add photos loads pictures from your device, or drop image files anywhere.
  • DOWNLOAD prints your wall. SHARE sends it to a friend.
  • ✕ removes one print. RESET clears the desk. Both can be undone.
  • Type pho anywhere for a little pho storm.

Photos never leave your browser. Your wall is kept for this visit only.
